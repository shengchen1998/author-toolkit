# Author Toolkit

Shared fiction creation and revision state uses one vocabulary for manuscript scopes, review evidence, and author decisions.

## Review and decisions

**Review brief**:
A summary of resolved and dismissed findings used to recognize previously decided observations.
_Avoid_: Reviewer context, fresh-reader brief

**Request review**:
A review of the text selected for a revision request and its affected passages.
_Avoid_: Diff approval, current-manuscript review
