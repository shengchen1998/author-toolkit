# Content Editing

Evaluate the teaching in the requested chapter or passage for clarity, completeness, and accuracy. Keep the author's promise, target reader, and transformation arc in view.

## Review

1. State the chapter's intended takeaway and what the reader should be able to do next.
2. Check clarity: identify undefined terms, missing steps, buried conclusions, and examples that do not demonstrate the claim.
3. Check completeness: compare the teaching with the prerequisites and promised outcome. Flag missing decisions, exceptions, or practice opportunities.
4. Check accuracy: distinguish claims supported by the manuscript from claims requiring external evidence. Ask the author to supply or verify sources; do not invent citations or present unchecked factual claims as verified.
5. Recommend focused changes and stop for author review. Structural changes return to the blueprint or transformation arc before prose revision.

Diagnostic findings use `content-editing` as the audit name and the specific teaching issue as the technique when a finding contract is requested. Generative lesson design remains outside the finding contract.
